## Words, with room to breathe

A good reading page should get out of the way. A comfortable line length, a clear rhythm, and just enough contrast to keep your place. **Emphasis should feel deliberate**, and *an aside should still sound like an aside*.

> The useful test is simple: does the page still feel good halfway through a long thought?
